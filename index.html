Supply Chain Control Tower

Demand forecasting carried all the way to an inventory policy, then tested in simulation on demand the models never saw. Four forecast models compete in a rolling backtest; safety stock is sized from the forecast's measured error and each supplier's lead-time reliability.

50% lower than a weeks-of-cover rule

Predictive maintenance

Railcar Force Anomaly Detection

My capstone with MxV Rail analysed 230,000+ wheel-force readings from six track sites to flag high-stress equipment for maintenance review. That work is under NDA, so this is an independent recreation of the approach on synthetic data I generate: ratio and trend metrics that cancel out load, speed and site so a growing wheel defect stands out.

Defects caught
84% vs 48% for a fixed force limit, at equal false alarms
Earlier warning
27 days sooner (median) when both fire
Data
Fully synthetic, 229k readings; no MxV data or code
